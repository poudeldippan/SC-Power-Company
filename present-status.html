Present Status & Progress

Home › Project › Present Status & Progress
The Tila-1 Hydropower Project (440 MW) has achieved major institutional, statutory, and technical milestones. With a formal Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) executed with NEA, approved Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA), and a 400 kV Grid Connection Agreement signed with RPGCL, the project is actively advancing consortium debt financing with lead arranger NMB Bank Ltd.

Historical & Forward Roadmap

Project Execution Timeline (2006 – 2030)

September 2006 (2063 B.S.)

Company Incorporation

S.C. Power Company Pvt. Ltd. was incorporated under the laws of Nepal (Reg No. 41031/063/064) with the mandate to pioneer mega-hydropower projects in Karnali Province.

November 2014 (2071 B.S.)

Environmental Clearance Approved

Ministry of Forests and Environment formally approved the comprehensive Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA), certifying social and ecological baseline mitigation.

October 2024 (2081 B.S.)

PPA Executed with NEA

S.C. Power Company signed the historic Power Purchase Agreement with Nepal Electricity Authority (NEA) for 298.75 MW peaking capacity, securing guaranteed long-term revenue off-take.

July 2026 (2083 B.S.)

Bank Consortium Site Inspection & Due Diligence

Consortium team led by NMB Bank Ltd. along with Prabhu Bank, NIC Asia Bank, and Employees Provident Fund completed detailed geotechnical and environmental site appraisals in Kalikot.

2026 – 2027

Financial Closure & EPC Contract Award

Execution of final consortium credit agreements and mobilization of international Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC) contractors for main civil and tunneling works.

December 2030 (2087 B.S.)

Commercial Operation Date (COD)

Successful synchronization with the 400 kV Karnali Corridor transmission line, delivering 440 MW of clean peaking hydroelectricity to the national and regional power grid.
