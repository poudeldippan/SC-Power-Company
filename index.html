Flagship Infrastructure

Tila-1 Hydropower Project (298.75 MW)

The Tila-1 Hydropower Project is a Peaking Run-of-River (PRoR) development situated along the Tila River in Kalikot District, Karnali Province. The project area is directly accessible via the Karnali Highway, providing dependable all-weather heavy logistical transport for electromechanical and civil plant equipment.

The civil scheme comprises a 69-meter high gated concrete gravity dam with seven spillway bays, underground desilting chambers engineered to extract quartz sediments, a 13.77 km headrace tunnel excavated via six strategic adits, and an underground powerhouse cavern (120.5 m × 18.5 m) accommodating four vertical-axis Francis turbines.

Designed for optimal peaking flexibility, Tila-1 operates with 6 hours of daily peak capacity, supplying essential stability and reserve power to the Nepal Electricity Authority (NEA) grid network.

Peaking Run-of-River Scheme

400 kV Karnali Corridor Evacuation

Direct power evacuation into the Rastriya Prasaran Grid Company Ltd (RPGCL) 400 kV Phukot Hub Substation, establishing seamless transmission across Nepal and cross-border corridors.

Zero household relocation or physical displacement required. Maintains a guaranteed downstream environmental flow of 2.03 m³/s, coupled with dedicated upstream fishway systems.
