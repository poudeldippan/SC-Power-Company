Company Profile

Home › About Us › Company Profile
S.C. Power Company Pvt. Ltd. (SCPCPL) is a premier Nepalese renewable energy enterprise established to develop high-capacity, grid-stabilizing hydroelectric assets. The company holds the mandate to build and operate the landmark 440 MW Tila-1 Peaking Run-of-River (PRoR) Hydropower Project in Kalikot District, Karnali Province.
Corporate Identity

Institutional Background & Incorporation

S.C. Power Company Pvt. Ltd. was incorporated under the Companies Act of Nepal on 2063/05/20 B.S. (September 5, 2006 A.D.) with Registration Number 41031/063/064 and Permanent Account Number (PAN) 302484080.

Over the past decade, the company has completed comprehensive hydrological monitoring, geological investigations (including exploratory adit tunnels and geophysical ERT surveys), Detailed Project Reports (DPR), and formal Environmental Impact Assessments (EIA).

Headquartered in Lalitpur with an operational field base in Manma, Kalikot, SCPCPL unites seasoned energy entrepreneurs, infrastructure investors, and distinguished former public administrators dedicated to bolstering Nepal's energy independence.

Guiding Principles

Core Values That Define Our Organization

Our governance framework is built on technical excellence, transparent investor alignment, and profound environmental responsibility.

Engineering Integrity

Rigorous engineering led by Shah Consult International ensures uncompromised underground safety across 13.77 km of tunnel networks and complex powerhouse caverns.

Environmental Stewardship

Comprehensive Environmental & Social Management Units (ESMU) enforce strict aquatic biodiversity protection and slope stabilization along the Karnali River basin.

Community Upliftment

Long-term socioeconomic commitment to Kalikot communities through local workforce prioritization, upgraded rural roads, and dedicated local equity share offerings.
