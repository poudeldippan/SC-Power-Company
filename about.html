Company Profile

Home › About Us › Company Profile
S.C. Power Company Pvt. Ltd. (SCPCPL) is a premier Nepalese renewable energy enterprise established to develop high-capacity, grid-stabilizing hydroelectric assets. The company holds the exclusive license and mandate to build and operate the landmark 298.75 MW Tila-1 Peaking Run-of-River (PRoR) Hydropower Project in Kalikot District, Karnali Province.
Corporate Identity

Institutional Background & Incorporation

S.C. Power Company Pvt. Ltd. was incorporated under the Companies Act of Nepal on 2063/05/20 B.S. (September 5, 2006 A.D.) with Registration Number 41031/063/064 and Permanent Account Number (PAN) 302484080.

The Department of Electricity Development (DoED), Ministry of Energy, Water Resources and Irrigation, issued Electricity Generation Survey License No. 294 authorizing the development of the Tila-1 project. Over the past decade and a half, the company has completed rigorous hydrological monitoring, geological investigations (including exploratory adit tunnels and ERT surveys), Detailed Project Reports (DPR), and formal Environmental Impact Assessments (EIA).

Headquartered in Lalitpur with an operational field base in Manma, Kalikot, SCPCPL unites seasoned energy entrepreneurs, infrastructure investors, and distinguished former public administrators dedicated to bolstering Nepal's energy independence.

Guiding Principles

Core Values That Define Our Organization

Engineering Integrity

Rigorous engineering led by Shah Consult International ensures uncompromised underground safety across 13.77 km of tunnel networks and complex powerhouse caverns.
