Vision, Mission & Objectives

Home › About Us › Vision, Mission & Objectives
At S.C. Power Company Pvt. Ltd., our vision and mission are rooted in a national commitment to decarbonization, energy security, and inclusive regional development. The 298.75 MW Tila-1 Project is engineered to serve as a flagship peaking facility along the Karnali River basin.
Our Long-Term Ambition

Corporate Vision

To be Nepal’s premier benchmark renewable energy developer, harnessing pristine Himalayan river systems through world-class engineering to provide reliable, clean, and dispatchable peaking energy that drives domestic industrialization and powers regional green economic integration across South Asia.

  • Delivering vital peaking power to stabilize the Integrated Nepal Power System (INPS).
  • Championing global Environmental, Social & Governance (ESG) standards.
  • Leading cross-border clean electricity export to neighboring South Asian markets.
Our Guiding Purpose

Corporate Mission

To construct, commission, and safely operate the 298.75 MW Tila-1 Peaking Run-of-River Hydropower Project ahead of the December 2030 Required Commercial Operation Date, while generating sustainable value for local communities in Kalikot, our consortium lending institutions, and our shareholders.

  • Executing underground works (13.77 km tunnel) with precision geotechnical safety.
  • Fostering socioeconomic upliftment through local job creation and equity participation.
  • Maintaining financial rigor and punctual debt service across consortium facilities.
Actionable Goals

Strategic Objectives (2026 - 2031)

Our roadmap focuses on five measurable strategic pillars designed to ensure execution success and long-term operating excellence.

1. Technical & Construction Milestones

Complete underground tunneling across all 6 adit access points, erect the 182.4 m concrete gravity dam, install 4x75 MW Pelton units, and achieve COD by December 2030.

2. 6-Hour Guaranteed Peaking Power

Utilize 1.7 MCM live reservoir storage on the Tila River to provide vital high-value electricity during morning and evening peak demand hours, displacing imported fossil fuel generation.

3. Financial Discipline & Consortium Strength

Maintain transparent financial management under the NMB Bank-led banking consortium, ensuring prudent drawdown schedules, interest capitalization, and robust project IRR.

4. Environmental & Social Governance (ESG)

Implement the approved Environmental Management Plan (EMP), safeguard aquatic habitats with mandatory downstream environmental discharge, and rehabilitate all spoil disposal areas.

5. Community & Local Equity Empowerment

Execute community infrastructure investments in health and education across Kalikot, and facilitate local share participation (IPO) to ensure project affected residents are co-owners.

6. 400 kV Karnali Corridor Power Evacuation

Synchronize with Rastriya Prasaran Grid Company Ltd (RPGCL) at the 400 kV Phukot Hub Substation via a dedicated 6 km double-circuit transmission line to deliver bulk power to national load centers.
