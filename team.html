Lead Technical Consultants & Partners

Shah Consult International (P.) Ltd.

Established in 1992, Shah Consult International is one of Nepal's most respected engineering and architectural consulting institutions. The firm serves as the lead engineering consultant conducting the comprehensive Due Diligence Study, technical design review, and optimization of the 298.75 MW Tila-1 DPR.

Earlier Detailed Project Reports were formulated in collaboration with SNC Lavalin Engineering India (2012), ensuring international best practices in rock mechanics, underground cavern sizing, and hydraulic transient design.
