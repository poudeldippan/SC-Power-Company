S.C. Power Company Executes 298.75 MW PPA with Nepal Electricity Authority

S.C. Power Company Pvt. Ltd. has formally executed a long-term Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) with the Nepal Electricity Authority (NEA) for the 298.75 MW Tila-1 Peaking Run-of-River Hydropower Project, providing guaranteed commercial take-or-pay off-take.
